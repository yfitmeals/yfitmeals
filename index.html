    FAQ

    Good to know

    When do I need to order by?

    Orders close every Friday at 9pm. Meals are ready for delivery or self-collection that Sunday at 2pm.

    How much does it cost?

    Menu A is $8 per meal. Menu B is $13 per meal, or $51 for 4 meals. Extra meat and rice is $2 per meal, and delivery is a flat $15. Self-collection is free.

    What is the free macro tracking?

    Every purchase comes with the calories, protein, carbs and fats for your meals, calculated to the gram. Easy to log, easy to stay on track.

    How do I pay?

    PayNow or QR to +65 9818 8807 with the remark “YF Prep”, then upload your payment screenshot when you place your order. Your order is confirmed once we receive payment.

    Does the menu change?

    Yes. There are two new rotating menus every week, so you get variety without having to think about it.

    How will I know my delivery is on the way?

    We’ll update you on WhatsApp once your meals are out for delivery.
