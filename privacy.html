Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short: we only collect what we need to prepare, deliver and confirm your meal order. We don’t sell your data or use it for anything else without your consent, and you can ask us to see, correct or delete it at any time.

YFIT Meals (“we”, “us”) is a home-based meal prep business in Singapore. This notice explains how we handle your personal data under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

This notice is available in English and Malay. If there is any difference between them, the English version applies.

What we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • your name and phone number
  • your email address, if you choose to give it
  • your delivery address, if you choose delivery
  • any notes you add, such as allergies or special requests
  • your order details and your PayNow payment screenshot

Why we collect it

We use your personal data only to:

  • prepare and confirm your order, and check your payment
  • deliver your meals or arrange self-collection
  • contact you about your order, for example on WhatsApp when your meals are on the way
  • handle any questions, feedback or issues with your order

We won’t send you marketing messages unless you’ve agreed to receive them, and you can opt out any time.

Your consent

By placing an order, you agree that we can collect, use and disclose your personal data for the purposes above. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. If you do, we may not be able to fulfil orders that are still in progress.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell, rent or trade your personal data. We only share it where it’s needed for your order:

  • Delivery: your name, phone number and address are given to the person delivering your order.
  • Services we use: orders are sent to us through WhatsApp, and may be stored in Google services (such as Google Sheets and Google Drive). These providers handle data under their own privacy terms.
  • Where required by law: we may disclose data if Singapore law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your order details only for as long as we need them to serve you and for our business records, which is no longer than 12 months after your last order. After that, we delete them.

How we protect it

Your order information is kept in accounts that only YFIT Meals can access, and we take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised access, use or disclosure.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it has been used
  • correct any personal data that’s wrong or out of date
  • delete your personal data, or stop using it

We’ll respond as soon as we reasonably can, usually within 30 days.

This website

This website doesn’t use tracking or advertising cookies. It only remembers your choice of light or dark mode and your language, and those settings stay on your own device. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may see your device’s IP address.
